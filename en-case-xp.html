Context

XP is a company that has been growing rapidly. As a result, a lot of content had to be added to the homepage — which can make navigation difficult for the user.

I created a proposal for a new menu and homepage to make navigation easier and ensure the right content reaches the user at the right moment.

  • Accelerated product growth
  • Excess content on the home
  • Fragmented navigation
The Challenge

Key question

How do we organize an expanding portfolio without distancing the user from what they actually need?

Approach

I reorganized the menu around simple categories and designed a homepage that prioritizes discovery — not the catalog. The goal is to guide users to the right product in just a few steps, with visual cues that reduce decision load.

Menu by category

Relevant information grouped into simple categories — users find what they need with ease.

Homepage with hierarchy

XP's main resources highlighted to make discovery of new content and services easier.

Interactive elements

Visual cues and interactions that help users find what they're looking for quickly.
