Case Study · SaaS Platform

INFLUO

Transforming creator marketing with artificial intelligence.

The Challenge

Finding the right creator is still a slow, manual, trial-and-error process. Existing tools require complex filters, but they ignore an essential point: users don't know exactly what to look for.

The question

How do we turn a complex problem into a simple, intelligent experience?

  • Slow
  • Manual
  • Trial and error
Discovery & Research

We started from a common hypothesis: users would find creators using filters. During interviews with marketers and small brands, we realized that users think in terms of goals, not parameters.

Key learnings:

"I know the campaign goal, but I don't know where to start."
"Filters don't guarantee good results."
"Testing creators takes too much time."

Insight

The problem isn't searching for creators — it's turning intent into decision.

The Solution

We changed the approach completely: from a filter-based search to an AI-guided experience. The user describes their campaign in natural language, and the system interprets intent, analyzes context, and recommends creators based on compatibility.

Interpretation

NLP to understand the campaign brief beyond keywords.

Context

Analysis of niche, audience, and real engagement against the goal.

Compatibility

Match score based on historical data and semantics.

Design decision

Turning search into a conversational experience reduced cognitive load, removed friction, and brought the system closer to the user's mental model.

Experience structure

  • Side menu for navigation
  • AI chat as primary entry point
  • Dynamic results alongside
Prototyping & Testing

The solution evolved in fast cycles — from filter-based search to chat with real-time results. We ran 4 qualitative tests with real users.

Test results

Preference for chat over filters 100%

Noticeable reduction in time to understand the interface. Increased confidence when the AI explained its recommendations.

Iterations

  • Reduced information on cards
  • Highlighted compatibility score
  • Improved spacing and readability
  • Simplified visual hierarchy
The Design Engineer's Role

I worked as Design Engineer across the entire process — discovery, research, UX/UI, interaction design, prototyping, and front-end development with AI. The integration between design and code allowed faster iterations and more precise decisions.

Engineering + Design

AI was used both in the product and in the construction of the interface, ensuring consistency and performance in the final experience.

Discovery Research UX/UI Prototyping Front-end with AI

Final result & impact

A platform that lets you discover creators using natural language, receive data-driven recommendations, and make decisions with more clarity.

Impact

  • Reduced complexity in discovery
  • Natural, intuitive experience
  • Foundation for intelligent matchmaking

Learning

"Designing for AI isn't about adding technology. It's about removing complexity."
