Context

Mobile redesign of the academic portal

Methodology

Qualitative research and Value Proposition Canvas

Platform

Mobile, smartphone-first

Overview

The PUC-SP academic portal had navigation problems, information that was hard to reach and a low usability perception among students. The starting point of the project was to listen, in the students' own voice, where the experience broke in their daily lives.

The goal of the redesign was to simplify access to the most-searched information: grades, financial status, schedule and contact channels with the university.

Premise

Students were unable to find critical academic information quickly.

Solution · Prototype

The prototype organizes everything that was scattered on the old portal into a clear navigation, with a hamburger menu and direct categories: Student, Course, Agenda, Financial, News and Contact.

  • Simple login with student name and RA
  • Home screen with priority news and notices
  • Financial section with monthly status and direct invoice generation

Agenda

  • Side menu organized by category, no deep submenus
  • In-app online support, without leaving the portal
  • Academic calendar with schedule and daily commitments

Design Decisions

Simplification

Fewer clicks to reach the information surfaced by the research.

Hierarchy

News, financial and agenda prioritized on the home and in the menu.

Accessibility

Mobile-first, generous touch targets and clear typographic contrast.

Organization

Six direct categories: Student, Course, Agenda, Financial, News and Contact.

Impact

The project turned a fragmented academic experience into a more accessible and organized digital platform for PUC-SP students.

  • Usability driven by real research findings
  • Cleaner and more direct information architecture
  • Faster access to the critical tasks of academic life
  • Navigation without noise, with well-defined categories
