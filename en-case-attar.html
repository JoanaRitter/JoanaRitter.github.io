Case Study · Mobile App

Attar

Professional relocation made agile and innovative.

Design Thinking UX Research Prototyping Mobile App Personas

Goal

Support professional relocation and career improvement

Methodology

Design Thinking · 4 phases

Product

Mobile platform with jobs, courses, and selection

Overview
EN-US

Searching for the ideal job and keeping up to date has never been more in demand than today.

We created this digital platform with several opportunities — free courses, professional and intellectual improvement, and direct routing to relevant jobs and selection processes.

Through this app, relocation to the job market has become more dynamic, faster and less stressful.

Objective: help people looking for professional improvement or a return to the job market in an agile and effective way.

Challenge: understand people's needs during this delicate period and get them to adopt an app that helps them in an innovative way.

Design Thinking was the methodology used — a structured approach to generate and improve ideas in four phases, from identifying the challenge to solving the problem.

PT-BR

Buscar o emprego ideal e manter-se atualizado nunca esteve tão em alta quanto nos dias de hoje.

Criamos uma plataforma digital com diversas oportunidades — divulgação de cursos gratuitos, aperfeiçoamento profissional e direcionamento para vagas e processos seletivos.

Através do aplicativo, a recolocação no mercado de trabalho se tornou mais dinâmica, rápida e menos estressante.

Objetivo: ajudar pessoas que buscam aperfeiçoamento ou recolocação no mercado de forma ágil e eficaz.

Desafio: entender as necessidades das pessoas durante esse período delicado e fazer com que elas adiram a um aplicativo capaz de auxiliá-las de forma inovadora.

Design Thinking foi a metodologia utilizada — uma abordagem estruturada para gerar e aprimorar ideias em quatro fases.

Research
EN / PT

We interviewed people who helped us measure which information and functions would be paramount for the application.

Based on the surveys, we created two personas — one with an entrepreneurial profile and ample experience, the other starting their professional life. Both represent our ideal customer and direct product construction.

Entrevistamos pessoas que nos ajudaram a mensurar quais informações e funções seriam primordiais para o aplicativo. Com base nas entrevistas, criamos duas personas: uma com perfil empreendedor e ampla vivência, outra iniciando a vida profissional.

Empathy Map
EN / PT

After creating the two personas, we made the empathy map so that we could, in a collaborative way, get to know our users in depth and have a real view of their behaviors, being able to delve into their pains and needs.
